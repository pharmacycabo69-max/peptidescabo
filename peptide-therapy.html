What Is Peptide Therapy?

Small molecules, targeted signals

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that occur naturally in the body and act as messengers — regulating everything from hormone release to fat metabolism and tissue repair. Peptide therapy uses carefully dosed, pharmacy-sourced peptides to support specific goals like fat loss, lean muscle preservation, and recovery, always under medical supervision.

Fat Metabolism

AOD-9604

A modified fragment of human growth hormone studied for its role in supporting fat breakdown without affecting blood sugar.

Body Composition

CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin

A growth-hormone-releasing peptide pairing often used to support lean body composition, sleep quality, and recovery.

Visceral Fat

Tesamorelin

A growth-hormone-releasing hormone analog studied specifically for its effect on visceral (abdominal) fat.

Metabolic Support

MOTS-c

A mitochondrial-derived peptide of interest for metabolic and exercise-performance support; more investigational than other options.

Research depth varies widely across these peptides — some are FDA-approved for specific conditions, others are used off-label or remain investigational. Your provider will walk through what's established and what's still emerging before recommending any protocol.

Is Peptide Therapy Right For You?

FAQ

Peptide therapy FAQs

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules in the body. Weight-loss-adjacent peptides like AOD-9604, CJC-1295, and Ipamorelin are used to support fat metabolism, lean body composition, and recovery, typically alongside — not instead of — core lifestyle and GLP-1 strategies.

Semaglutide and tirzepatide are FDA-approved GLP-1 medications with large-scale clinical trial data behind them. Many other peptides are used off-label or are considered investigational, with a smaller body of research. Your provider will explain what's established versus emerging for any peptide you're considering.

Some patients use targeted peptides alongside GLP-1 therapy under close provider supervision. This is always evaluated individually — never assume combination therapy is appropriate without a consultation.

Most weight-loss and recovery peptides are given as a small subcutaneous injection, similar to semaglutide or tirzepatide, following a schedule set by your provider.

Sourcing matters. We work only with licensed compounding pharmacies, and every peptide protocol is reviewed by a licensed medical provider before it's prescribed. As with any treatment, candidacy and appropriateness are assessed on an individual basis.
