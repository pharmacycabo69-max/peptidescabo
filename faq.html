Semaglutide & Tirzepatide

Clinical trials report average losses of roughly 15% of body weight with semaglutide and up to 20% with tirzepatide over 52–68 weeks. Individual results vary.

Both are well-studied GLP-1 medications. As with any prescription treatment, candidacy is confirmed through a consultation that screens for contraindications like a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or pancreatitis.

Your care team will give you clear guidance on missed doses during onboarding; general guidance depends on how long it's been since your scheduled injection, so always check with your provider.

Peptide Therapy

We offer AOD-9604, CJC-1295/Ipamorelin, tesamorelin, and MOTS-c, among others, depending on your goals and candidacy.

Some peptides have FDA approval for specific conditions; others are used off-label or remain investigational. Your provider will explain the evidence behind any protocol before you start.
