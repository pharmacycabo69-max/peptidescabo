What are GLP-1 weight loss medications?

Semaglutide and tirzepatide belong to a class of medications called GLP-1 receptor agonists. They mimic a natural gut hormone that signals fullness to your brain, slows digestion, and helps regulate blood sugar — which is why they were originally developed for type 2 diabetes before clinical trials showed significant weight-loss benefits.
